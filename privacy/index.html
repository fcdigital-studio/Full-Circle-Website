Last updated: 4 June 2026

Who we are

Full Circle provides marketing strategy, media buying, campaign reporting, and recruitment campaign support for client projects.

Information we collect

We may collect information that people submit through campaign forms, landing pages, emails, or recruitment application flows, including name, phone number, email address, location, qualification, work experience, and application notes.

How we use information

  • To manage campaign reporting and performance analysis.
  • To route recruitment applications to the relevant client team.
  • To communicate with clients and applicants when required for the campaign.
  • To improve campaign quality, tracking, and operational follow-up.

Sharing information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with the relevant client, recruitment team, advertising platform, or service provider only when needed to operate, report, or improve the campaign.

Data retention

We retain campaign and application information only for as long as needed for reporting, recruitment review, client communication, or legal and operational requirements.
